Method

  • Each poem is parsed as one text with spaCy; line numbers are kept per token. Every verb, and every be that is not an auxiliary, becomes a relation.
  • Agent = grammatical subject of an active verb (actor or experiencer), or the by-phrase of a passive. Patient = direct object, passive subject, or prepositional object of believe in, yearn for, gnaw at, open upon. Other prepositional phrases are listed as obliques. Clausal objects (“thinks it is life”) are recorded as clauses, and the embedded clause becomes a relation of its own.
  • Rules supply implicit subjects: infinitives take the subject of the governing verb (“dares to exist”); -ing participles take the noun they modify as agent (“blue moving”) and -ed participles take it as patient (“deified temple”); coordinated verbs share a subject; relative pronouns resolve to their antecedent; gapped relatives supply objects (“the pebbles you tumble about”). “self-guided, self-oiled” count as reflexive passives, and imperatives take the addressee as agent.
  • Pronoun links that need interpretation, participant assignments, verb kinds and all corrections are tables in build_agency.py (PRONOUNS, LEXICON, ENTITIES, VERB_KIND_OF, CORRECTIONS, ADDITIONS, AMBIGUITIES). Edit and re-run to rebuild this page. Anything drawn from the hand tables carries a badge: pronoun by handcorrectedaddedrejected.
  • Descriptions (DESCRIPTIONS) record phrases that characterise a participant without being clauses: appositives, similes, metaphors, catalogues and attributes. Each links to what it describes, plus alternative attachments where the grammar allows them. They are not counted as agency. An agent noun such as “witness” is also entered as an action (ADDITIONS).
  • Graph: nodes are entities (colour = type, size = participation). Arrows run from agent to patient, coloured by verb kind and thickened by frequency. Self-loops are reflexives. Short outward arrows are actions with no patient, and dashed arrows are copulas (X is Y). Click an arrow or node to see its lines.
